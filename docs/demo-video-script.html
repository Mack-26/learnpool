Horizon — Sales Demo Video

School Administrators Cut

Core message: Your students are already using AI to study. Right now you have zero visibility into it. Horizon gives you that visibility, grounded in your own faculty's materials.

Runtime 90–120s Audience Deans / provosts / academic affairs Goal Booked demo
Why this angle — admins don't buy features, they buy risk reduction and oversight. Two questions decide whether they take the meeting:
  1. Academic integrity — is the AI trustworthy, grounded in our curriculum, not the open internet?
  2. Visibility — can faculty and administration see what's happening, or is this shadow-IT like students privately using ChatGPT?
The whole script answers both in under two minutes, then asks for the meeting. No social-learning framing, no feature tour — this replaces the earlier longer, student-facing cut for a cold administrator audience.
