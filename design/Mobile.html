If x + 1/x = 5, what is x² + 1/x²?

9 votes3 replies2 forks
Horizon from your materials

You don't need x. Square what you were given — the cross terms collapse:

(x + 1/x)² = x² + 2 + 1/x² = 25
x² + 1/x² = 23
