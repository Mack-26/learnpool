Why does water move toward the saltier side in osmosis?

Horizon From Lecture 9 slides and the Ch. 5 reading

Water doesn't move toward the salt — it moves toward wherever there is less free water, and dissolved salt is what lowers it. Both sides keep exchanging water, but the side with more solute has fewer water molecules free to leave, so the net flow runs toward it until the concentrations even out. [1]

On the exam this is worded as movement from low to high solute concentration. [2]
