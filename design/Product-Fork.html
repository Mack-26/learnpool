Frobenius & spectral norms

3 exchanges · last worked on this morning
You asked

Does the same invariance hold for the spectral norm?

Horizon Same materials as the group

Yes. Both norms are unitarily invariant, because both are built only from the singular values, and orthogonal factors don’t change those. The difference is how much they keep: the spectral norm reads off σ₁ alone, while the Frobenius norm sums all of them. [1]

‖A‖₂ = σ₁     ‖A‖F = √(σ₁² + … + σₕ²)
